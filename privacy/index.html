This policy explains what information the Warlot mobile game (“the game”, package com.warlot.game) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The game is developed by Lunar Meridian, an independent developer (“we”, “us”).

1. Who is responsible

The data controller for the information described here is Gizem Nihal Boyraz, an independent developer publishing under the name Lunar Meridian (Türkiye). Contact: lunarmeridian.studio@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We do not ask for your email address, phone number, real name, contacts, photos or precise location.

3. Who processes your information

We use these service providers. They process information on our behalf and under their own privacy terms:

  • Google (Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, AdMob, User Messaging Platform, Firebase, Play Integrity) – policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Friends, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Matchmaker, Lobby, Relay) – unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We do not sell your personal information. Your in-game name, profile summary and online status are visible to other players as described above.

4. Advertising and consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game asks for your consent or offers an opt-out before showing personalised ads, using Google’s consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy options. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

5. International transfers

Google and Unity may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union, under their standard data protection safeguards.

6. How long we keep information

  • Online account, friends and public profile: until you delete your online account.
  • Match records: as long as needed to verify results and investigate cheating, at most 12 months.
  • Player reports: up to 12 months for safety and moderation, also after account deletion.
  • Analytics data: according to the retention setting in Google Analytics (up to 14 months). Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Progress on your device: until you uninstall the game. Google Play Games saved games and achievements are managed by Google under your Google account.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your online account: in the game, Settings ▸ “Delete my online account”, or see warlotgame.com/delete-account.
  • Change ad consent in Settings ▸ Privacy options; reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Block or report players from their profile.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye’s KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your information. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Game results that affect other players are verified on the server.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will also be announced in the game.

6. Saklama süreleri

  • Çevrim içi hesap, arkadaşlar ve herkese açık profil: çevrim içi hesabını silene kadar.
  • Maç kayıtları: sonucun doğrulanması ve hile incelemesi için gerektiği kadar, en çok 12 ay.
  • Oyuncu bildirimleri: güvenlik ve denetim için en çok 12 ay; hesap silindikten sonra da.
  • Analiz verileri: Google Analytics’teki saklama ayarına göre (en çok 14 ay). Çökme raporları: en çok 90 gün.
  • Cihazındaki ilerleme: oyunu kaldırana kadar. Google Play Games kayıtlı oyunları ve başarımları Google hesabın altında Google tarafından yönetilir.

7. Seçeneklerin ve hakların

  • Çevrim içi hesabını sil: oyunda Ayarlar ▸ “Çevrim içi hesabımı sil”, ya da warlotgame.com/delete-account.
  • Reklam onayını Ayarlar ▸ Gizlilik seçenekleri’nden değiştir; reklam kimliğini cihaz ayarlarından sıfırla.
  • Oyuncuları profillerinden engelle ya da bildir.
  • Bulunduğun yere göre (ör. KVKK ya da GDPR kapsamında) bilgilerine erişme, düzeltme, silme ve işlenmesine itiraz etme hakların olabilir. Bize e-posta gönder; 30 gün içinde cevap veririz.
