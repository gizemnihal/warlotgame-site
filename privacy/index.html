This policy explains what information the Warlot mobile game (“the game”, package com.warlot.game) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The game is developed by Lunar Meridian, an independent developer (“we”, “us”).

1. Who is responsible

The data controller for the information described here is Gizem Nihal Boyraz, an independent developer publishing under the name Lunar Meridian (Türkiye). Contact: lunarmeridian.studio@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We do not ask for your email address, phone number, real name, contacts, photos or precise location. The game has no voice chat and no free-text chat.

3. Who processes your information

We use these service providers. They process information on our behalf and under their own privacy terms:

  • Google (Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing and the Google Play Developer API, AdMob, User Messaging Platform, Firebase including Cloud Messaging, Play Integrity) – policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Friends, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards, Matchmaker, Lobby, Relay, Remote Config, Vivox messaging) – unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We do not sell your personal information. Your in-game name, profile summary and online status are visible to other players as described above.

4. Advertising and consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game asks for your consent or offers an opt-out before showing personalised ads, using Google’s consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy options. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.

5. International transfers

Google and Unity may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union, under their standard data protection safeguards.

6. How long we keep information

  • Online account, progress, purchases delivered to your account, friends and public profile: until you delete your account.
  • Match records: as long as needed to verify results and investigate cheating, at most 12 months, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Player reports: up to 12 months for safety and moderation, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • After account deletion we keep only an anonymous purchase record (see section 7).
  • Analytics data: according to the retention setting in Google Analytics (up to 14 months). Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Device settings: until you uninstall the game.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the game from Settings ▸ Delete my account, or as described at warlotgame.com/delete-account. Deletion is immediate and removes your player data on our server (wallet, owned items, progress, league and season records, profile and player name), your leaderboard entries, friend relationships, invitation and report records, your match records, the game save on your device and your Google Play Games cloud save. Device settings (such as sound and language) stay on your device. An ongoing ranked match that affects your opponent’s result is kept only until that match is resolved, so your opponent can receive their result.

Records kept after deletion. To prevent fraud and the use of the same purchase on more than one account, and to handle refunds and payment disputes, we keep a one-way hash of the purchase token, the product ID and the delivery time. This record cannot be linked to the deleted account and does not identify you. Permanent items you paid for on Google Play (such as theme packs) can be restored to a new game account with the same Google account.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time (section 7).
  • Change ad consent in Settings ▸ Privacy options; reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Block or report players from their profile. Turn off quick messages and announcement notifications in Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye’s KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your information. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

9. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Progress, purchases and game results that affect other players are verified on the server.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will also be announced in the game.

6. Saklama süreleri

  • Çevrim içi hesap, ilerleme, hesabına teslim edilen satın almalar, arkadaşlar ve herkese açık profil: hesabını silene kadar.
  • Maç kayıtları: sonucun doğrulanması ve hile incelemesi için gerektiği kadar, en çok 12 ay; hesabını silince silinir.
  • Oyuncu bildirimleri: güvenlik ve denetim için en çok 12 ay; hesabını silince silinir.
  • Hesap silindikten sonra yalnız anonim bir satın alma kaydı saklanır (bkz. 7. bölüm).
  • Analiz verileri: Google Analytics’teki saklama ayarına göre (en çok 14 ay). Çökme raporları: en çok 90 gün.
  • Cihaz ayarları: oyunu kaldırana kadar.

7. Hesabının silinmesi

Hesabını oyunda Ayarlar ▸ Hesabımı sil ile ya da warlotgame.com/delete-account sayfasındaki yolla silebilirsin. Silme anında yapılır; sunucudaki oyuncu verilerin (cüzdan, sahip olduğun ürünler, ilerleme, lig ve sezon kayıtları, profil ve oyuncu adı), sıralama tablolarındaki girdilerin, arkadaşlık ilişkilerin, davet ve şikâyet kayıtların, maç kayıtların, cihazındaki oyun kaydı ve Google Play Games bulut kaydın silinir. Cihaz ayarların (ses, dil gibi) cihazında kalır. Sürmekte olan ve rakibinin sonucunu etkileyen bir dereceli maç, rakibin sonucunu alabilmesi için yalnız o maç sonuçlanana kadar tutulur.

Silmeden sonra saklanan kayıt. Dolandırıcılığı ve aynı satın almanın birden fazla hesapta kullanılmasını önlemek, iade ve ödeme itirazlarını yönetmek için satın alma jetonunun tek yönlü özeti, ürün kimliği ve teslim zamanı saklanır. Bu kayıt silinen hesapla ilişkilendirilemez ve seni tanımlamaz. Google Play’de parasını ödediğin kalıcı ürünler (ör. tema paketleri) aynı Google hesabıyla yeni oyun hesabına geri yüklenebilir.
